<h2 align="center">What it does</h2>

<table>
  <tr>
    <td valign="top" width="50%">
      <h3>👻 Ghost database</h3>
      Every ghost on its own card with evidence, speed and hunt threshold. <b>Flip a card</b> to read its 0-evidence guide.
    </td>
    <td valign="top" width="50%">
      <h3>🔍 Evidence advisor</h3>
      Mark evidence as <b>found</b> or <b>ruled out</b> and see which ghosts are left, plus what to test next.
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td valign="top">
      <h3>⏱ Hunt tools</h3>
      Smudge timers, a hunt and cooldown tracker, a ghost <b>speed detector</b> and a notepad for the investigation.
    </td>
    <td valign="top">
      <h3>🪟 Mini map player</h3>
      A floating map window you can drag and resize while you browse, or pop out <i>above other windows</i> (Chrome and Edge).
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td valign="top">
      <h3>🗺 Maps</h3>
      Every map and floor as a full layout you can <b>zoom</b>, <b>pan</b> and view in fullscreen.
    </td>
    <td valign="top">
      <h3>☠️ Cursed items</h3>
      How the seven cursed possessions work, what they cost in sanity and when they trigger a hunt.
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td valign="top">
      <h3>🧰 Gear &amp; glossary</h3>
      What every tool does, and Phasmophobia terms explained in plain words.
    </td>
    <td valign="top">
      <h3>⌨️ Built for play</h3>
      Keyboard shortcuts for mid-hunt use (press <kbd>?</kbd> to see them), favorites and notes saved in your browser, and a boot-sequence intro.
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>
